Our story

Most childcare software bills the way enterprise software does: per location, per administrator, per parent login. Providers end up paying for their own growth twice. Binnii started from the other end — the only number that reflects the care actually being given is the number of children in your rooms today. So that is the only number on the bill.

We build in Canada, for Canadian providers first, with subsidy handling, tax receipts and CAD pricing designed in from the start rather than translated in later.

What we believe

Fair pricing

A home daycare and a ten-center group run the same product. Plans differ only by how many active children are included — features are never the lever.

Safety is not an upsell

Multi-factor authentication and audit trails belong on every plan. We will not gate a security feature behind a bigger invoice.

Your data belongs to you

Export anytime, in ordinary formats. If you stop paying, your account becomes read-only with clear notice — we never delete operational records without warning.
