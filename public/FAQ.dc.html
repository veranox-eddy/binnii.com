What counts as an “active child”?

A child who is enrolled, whose start date has arrived, and who hasn’t yet withdrawn or graduated. We count daily and deduplicate across all your centers — a child attending two of your programs is billed once. Applicants and waitlisted children are never billed.

Do you charge per center or per staff account?

No. One subscription covers your whole organization — locations, classrooms, staff logins and parent accounts are all unlimited and free. Adding a center never adds a location fee; new centers inherit your existing plan and billing cycle.

How does the annual discount work?

Annual plans prepay twelve months of base fee at 20% off — $96, $288 or $672 CAD. Overage is still settled monthly, at overage rates also discounted 20%: $0.80, $0.64 or $0.48 per child-month.

Is a child on vacation still billed?

Yes — short absences, sick days and vacations don’t change enrollment, so the child still counts. Part-time children count as one, not a fraction. Billing follows enrollment, which keeps every invoice explainable.

What happens when my trial ends?

If you’ve added a verified payment method, your subscription starts on the next billing cycle. If not, your account becomes read-only — you can still sign in, view and export everything, and pick a plan whenever you’re ready. Nothing is deleted without notice.

What happens if a payment fails?

Service continues while we notify you and retry — a full grace period before anything is restricted, and clear notices at every step. We never delete children’s records over a failed payment.

Can I export my data?

Yes — usage, invoices and operational reports export to CSV, PDF and Excel at any time, on every plan, including during a read-only period.

What payment methods do you accept?

Visa, Mastercard, American Express and recurring-capable debit cards, processed by Stripe. Canadian customers can also pay by Interac e-Transfer. Prices are in CAD and exclude applicable GST/HST/PST.
