Every plan includes every feature. Which plan is cheapest depends only on headcount: up to 30 children, Go; 31–90, Plus; 91 and up, Pro.

What counts as an active child

A child enrolled and started, not yet withdrawn — counted each day and deduplicated across all your centers, so a child attending two programs is billed once. Part-time or away on vacation still counts as one; applicants and waitlisted children are never billed.

How the bill is built

The base fee is charged up front; any overage is averaged from daily counts and lands on the next invoice. One bill per organization — adding a center never adds a location fee. Annual plans prepay the base at 20% off and discount the overage rate the same way.
